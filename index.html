About

I'm a security engineer who works best where there's little in place yet. I've spent most of my career in high-growth and cloud-native companies, building detection pipelines, security data lakes, and infrastructure-as-code guardrails, then scaling them as the organization grows.

My background started in enterprise networking (PKI, AAA, network access control) at critical-infrastructure scale, and moved up through cloud, containers, and identity. That depth helps me make sound architecture calls and scope incidents quickly. I write production code, treat detections like software, and partner closely with engineering teams so security keeps pace with the product.
